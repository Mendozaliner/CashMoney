## RESEARCH — Session 22 Experiments

### E31: CAPE Value Tilt on v2 → **DISCARDED** (watch-listed)
**Philosophy (Shiller, 1981–2015):** The Cyclically Adjusted P/E (CAPE) predicts 10-year real equity returns. At extreme valuations (top decile, CAPE ≥ 27), forward returns are compressed. A mild downside tilt on v2 exposure — not a binary off-switch (which E4/E8 proved harmful) — should reduce drawdown during extended overvaluation.

**What we found:**
- Best config `CT(lo0.50,t85)`: mean WF Sharpe **1.001** (vs v2 0.844), worst DD **−12.05%** (vs v2 −20.5%)
- DSR **0.988** ≥ 0.95 ✓ | CI **[−0.35, +1.11]** straddles zero ✗
- OOS correlation to v2: **1.000** (perfect)

**Root cause of failure:** CAPE has been ≥ 85th percentile for essentially the *entire* 2000–2023 modern era. The "tilt" is not timing — it's a constant 50% scaling of v2 for ~23 years. Constant scaling improves DD (less exposure) and Sharpe (T-bill on cash sleeve) but cannot clear the diff-vs-SPY CI because the *only* change is holding 50% less equity and 50% more cash at all times. This is the exact failure mode the skeptical prior predicted.

**Note on CAPE data:** Shiller data in cache ends September 2023 (CAPE = 30.8, p95 of full history). The 34-month gap is forward-filled. Real CAPE ~38-42 as of July 2026 (still top-decile). Tilt remains ACTIVE in the gap period.

**Watch-list:** Yes. Best DD (−12.05%) beats v2 by 8.4 percentage points — meaningful for capital-preservation mandates. Watch-list condition: if a new sleeve with low correlation to v2 emerges, CAPE tilt could serve as the low-risk overlay.

### E32: Equity Risk Premium (ERP) Carry Signal → **DISCARDED**
**Philosophy (Yardeni 1999, Asness 2003, Ilmanen 2011 AQR):** The ERP (equity earnings yield = 1/CAPE, minus T-bill rate) measures whether equities offer a carry advantage over cash. When ERP < 0 (equities offer less yield than T-bills), reduce v2 exposure. Unlike E31 (absolute CAPE level), E32 is rate-adjusted: it fired in 2022-23 when the Fed hiked T-bills from 0% to 4.5%, even when CAPE was "only" 28.

**What we found:**
- Best config `ERP(lo0.50,erp0.02)`: mean WF Sharpe **0.902** (vs v2 0.844), worst DD **−20.49%** (no improvement)
- DSR **0.976** ≥ 0.95 ✓ | CI **[−0.54, +0.94]** straddles zero ✗
- OOS correlation to v2: **0.948**

**Root cause of failure:** Current ERP = 1/30.8 − 3.8% = **−0.56%** (earnings yield below T-bill). ERP-based reduction fires in extended QE-era periods AND post-hike periods, both of which had mixed equity outcomes. The signal reduces exposure but cannot consistently time the exact drawdown windows — equities outperformed even at negative ERP during 2017-2021. CI straddles zero.
