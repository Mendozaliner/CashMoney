## New Analytical Tools

### tools/regime_detector.py — Dalio 4-Regime Economic Machine

Classifies each trading day into one of four regimes (bull_goldilocks, bull_inflation, bear_deflation,
bear_stagflation) using Shiller real EPS growth (growth axis) and rolling stock-bond correlation
(inflation axis). Current regime: **bear_stagflation** (EPS growth positive but below typical,
SB corr −0.24, negative → deflation inflation axis reading, but combined classification shows
stagflation — the system is sensitive at current thresholds).

Regime distribution (2000-2026): bull_goldilocks 43.7%, bear_deflation 25.3%, bear_stagflation 15.7%,
bull_inflation 15.4%.

### tools/factor_scores.py — Graham/Lynch/Buffett/AQR Scores

Four market-level factor scores (all [0,1] normalised except ERP):
- **Graham safety** (CAPE-based): 0.21 — market expensive (CAPE ~41 >> safe 15)
- **ERP / Buffett** (1/CAPE − rf): 0.21 — low equity risk premium vs current rates
- **GARP / Lynch** (EPS growth / CAPE): 0.13 — low growth quality score
- **Momentum / Carhart** (12-1 month): 0.70 — strong positive momentum
- **Composite**: 0.31 — below neutral (0.50); expensive market with good momentum

Interpretation: market is expensive by fundamental measures but momentum remains positive. This is
the typical late-cycle configuration — consistent with current bear_stagflation regime classification.

### tools/risk_metrics.py — Advanced Portfolio Risk Measures

Implements CVaR (Artzner 1999), Omega ratio (Shadwick & Keating 2002), Calmar ratio (Young 1991),
Information ratio (Grinold & Kahn 1999), and Tail ratio. Used in `comprehensive_risk_report()`.
