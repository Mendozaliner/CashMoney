## RESEARCH — E40: Variance Risk Premium (VRP) Timing

**Hypothesis (pre-registered as `41796c6396`):** The VRP (VIX² − realized_var) is a
statistically significant predictor of near-term equity returns (Bollerslev et al. 2009).
When VRP is BELOW its rolling median (complacency), scaling down v2 exposure by `vrp_scale`
should improve risk-adjusted returns. Distinct from E2 (VIX level) and E29 (VIX regime).

**Grid:** vrp_lb ∈ {40, 63} × vrp_scale ∈ {0.50, 0.75} — 4 configs

**Best config:** VRP(lb40, sc0.75) — end$1k $6,232 vs v2 $9,199 (−32% terminal value)

**VERDICT: DISCARDED** — Mean WF OOS 0.752 < 0.851 v2 bar. CI straddles zero.
corr_to_v2 = 0.991 (essentially a weaker v2).

**Root cause:** VRP "complacency" (VRP below rolling median) fires during calm bull
markets — exactly when buy-and-hold earns the most. The SMA200 trend gate in v2 already
handles true bear markets; the VRP overlay only trims exposure in benign periods
where full investment pays off best. Result: gives up upside without capturing any
additional downside protection.

**Not permanently closed.** The economic mechanism is real; the binary regime flag
and symmetric scaling are the failure mode. Future angle: continuous VRP z-score
weighting, or VRP combined with realized-vol acceleration.

`research/run_2026_08_01_s26_vrp_timing.py`
