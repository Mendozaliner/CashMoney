**E31 — CAPE / Shiller Value Tilt (Shiller 1988 / Campbell & Shiller 1988):**
When the CAPE is in the top 10% of its expanding-window distribution (current
CAPE ~30.8 per Sep 2023 data), v2 exposure is reduced by 35%. The results are
striking: all 6 configs beat v2's mean WF Sharpe (best 0.937 vs v2 0.851), and
worst DD drops from −20.5% to −13.7%. However, the CI straddles zero and
**corr_v2 = 1.000** in OOS — the CAPE has been above the 90th percentile
continuously since ~2017, making the tilt a constant 35% exposure reduction in
the OOS window. The strategy literally IS v2 × 0.65 in the 2020-2025 period.
Terminal value $5,809 vs v2 $8,382 confirms: the tilt reduces wealth by
underexposing in the ongoing bull market. **DISCARDED** (CI fails). The CAPE
concept is NOT closed — the signal works at decade horizons and should be
explored differently. This specific grid is exhausted; ≤12 configs spent.

**E32 — Yield Curve Regime Overlay (Harvey 1988 / Estrella & Mishkin 1998):**
IEF (7-10y) vs SHY (1-3y) relative momentum as a proxy for yield-curve slope.
When the short end outperforms the long end, the curve is flattening/inverting;
v2 exposure is scaled back by 25-50%. Best result: YC(lb63,s0.75) mean_wf 0.865,
DD −20.0%. However, CI straddles zero and **corr_v2 = 0.990** — the highest
correlation outside the VIX and inter-market experiments. Root cause: the
2022-2024 yield curve inversion was the longest in modern history (22 months),
but equities rallied 25%+ over that period. The OOS (2020-2025H) window contains
this regime and severely punishes the signal. The yield curve signal works at
4-8 QUARTER horizons, not at the daily equity-return frequency the engine models.
**DISCARDED and PERMANENTLY CLOSED** — yield curve ETF relative momentum is
structurally absorbed by v2's SMA200 gate (both respond to the same macro cycle).

**E33 — Tactical Bond Cash Sleeve (Faber 2007 / Hurst et al. 2017):**
The most promising new result this session. When v2 is in cash (SPY below
SMA200 band), idle capital is deployed into IEF whenever IEF is above its own
SMA200 trend gate. The equity signal is UNCHANGED. Result: TB(w200,f1.0)
achieves mean_wf 0.882, DD −20.2%, and grows $1k to **$10,467** — $2,085 (+25%)
more wealth than v2 over 26 years. The mechanism works as expected: in equity
bear markets (when v2 is in cash), bond ETFs typically rally; the IEF SMA200
gate protected against the 2022 rising-rate crash. However, **CI straddles zero**
— the 5-year OOS window lacks statistical power to detect the improvement.
**DISCARDED** (CI fails). Watch-list ineligible (corr 0.975 > 0.50 threshold).
This experiment is NOT permanently closed — the economic case is real (25% more
wealth) and a longer live track will eventually generate sufficient data for the
CI to clear zero. Flag as a capital-preservation and return-enhancement candidate
for future Phase-3 revisit.
