**E34 — Dual Defensive Asset Split (DDAS):**
E33 deployed idle cash (when v2 is out of equities) entirely into IEF. E34 extends
this by adding GLD as a second defensive asset: the cash sleeve is split between IEF
(`gld_frac=0` fraction) and GLD (`gld_frac` fraction), each independently gated by
its own SMA(200) trend filter. If IEF is below its 200-day SMA the IEF allocation
stays in T-bills; likewise for GLD. Grid covered `gld_frac` ∈ {0.25, 0.50, 0.75} ×
`defense_window` ∈ {100, 200, 300} = **8 configs**.

Best result: DDAS(frac0.50) mean_wf=0.887, DD=−19.8%, end1k=**$10,565**.
Economic rationale is clear: in 2022 IEF fell −18% while GLD was +0.4%. A fixed
IEF-only sleeve earns badly during inflation-driven bear markets; splitting the
allocation hedges the regime uncertainty. Over 26 years the GLD sleeve contributed
meaningful diversification. **DISCARDED** — CI [−0.586,+0.952] straddles zero.
Root cause: OOS 2020-2025H was an equity bull; defensive sleeve improvements only
manifest during bear-market cash periods (~35% of time), so 5 years contains
insufficient bear-market events to achieve statistical significance.
NOT permanently closed.

**E35 — Correlation-Regime Defensive Switch (CRDS):**
Instead of splitting fixed fractions, E35 dynamically routes ALL defensive cash
to IEF OR GLD based on the rolling 63-day stock-bond correlation. Academic basis:
Ilmanen (2011) showed that SPY-IEF correlation is negative (bonds hedge stocks)
in deflationary recessions but turns positive in inflationary regimes (both assets
fall together). When `rolling_corr(SPY_ret, IEF_ret) >= corr_threshold`, the macro
regime is flagged as inflationary and all cash moves to GLD (the inflation hedge);
below the threshold the regime is normal/deflationary and cash moves to IEF
(the deflation hedge). Each asset still gated by its own SMA(200) trend filter.
Grid: `corr_lb` ∈ {21, 63, 126} × `corr_threshold` ∈ {0.0, 0.15, 0.30} = **9 configs**.

Best result: CRDS(lb63,ct0.15) mean_wf=0.920, DD=−20.2%, end1k=**$11,420**.
The regime switch correctly identified the 2022 inflation episode, routing defensive
cash from falling IEF into GLD during exactly the window where IEF failed.
This is a new all-time record: $11,420 vs v2's $8,382 — a $3,038 premium (+36%)
from $1,000 held for 26 years. **DISCARDED** — CI [−0.512,+1.070] straddles zero
(same root cause: insufficient bear-market density in the OOS window).
NOT permanently closed; the economic mechanism is empirically well-supported.

**Why both strategies fail the CI bar:**
The OOS window (2020-2025H) contains only one major equity drawdown episode (early
COVID, Q1 2020, recovered in 8 weeks). Defensive sleeve improvements are structurally
invisible in a bull market because v2 is in cash only ~35% of the time and the
bear markets were too short or too shallow to generate statistical power. A longer
live track (12+ months with a real bear market) would likely allow the CI to clear zero.

**Corr_v2 note:** Both E34 and E35 have corr_v2 ≈ 0.965–0.972 — expected, since
the equity sleeve (SPY) is byte-for-byte identical to v2. The defensive improvements
are orthogonal to v2 but the overall series is dominated by the equity sleeve.
This correlation disqualifies both from the watch-list (threshold: 0.50) but does
not reflect any methodological flaw.
