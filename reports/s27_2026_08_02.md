## Research — E41: Minimum Variance Portfolio (Markowitz 1952)

**Philosophy tested:** Harry Markowitz's 1952 mean-variance framework. Allocate
across SPY, IEF, GLD by minimising portfolio variance using a rolling 60 or
120-day empirical covariance matrix. The first experiment in this project to use the
full Σ matrix rather than per-asset 1/σ weights (risk parity, E12).

**Grid:** cov_lookback ∈ {60, 120} × sma_gate ∈ {True, False} = 4 configs.
Total cumulative configs: 261.

| Config | Fold1 IS | Fold2 OOS | Fold3 OOS | Mean WF | MaxDD | End$1k |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MV(lb60, gate=T) | 1.045 | 1.196 | 0.338 | 0.767 | −20.7% | $3,312 |
| **MV(lb60, gate=F)** | **0.960** | **1.448** | **0.327** | **0.887** | **−20.1%** | **$3,251** |
| MV(lb120, gate=T) | 1.064 | 1.186 | 0.329 | 0.757 | −21.0% | $3,268 |
| MV(lb120, gate=F) | 0.919 | 1.456 | 0.315 | 0.885 | −19.8% | $3,160 |

**Best: MV(lb60, gate=F)** — mean WF OOS 0.887 BEATS the v2 bar (0.851).

| Gate | Avg SPY | Avg IEF | Avg GLD |

**Significance evaluation:**
- CI vs SPY: **[−0.049, +0.844]** — straddles zero → FAILS criterion

- Ensemble eligible: YES (corr 0.267 < 0.50 threshold)

**VERDICT: DISCARDED** — CI fails the significance gate.

**Root cause:** MinVar allocates ~62% to IEF on average. IEF earned roughly 2–3%/yr
over 2000–2026 while SPY earned ~8.4%/yr. Allocating 62% to IEF costs approximately
3.6% CAGR relative to v2 — the entire Sharpe advantage ($3,251 vs $9,199). The
MinVar signal IS correct in risk-adjusted terms (0.904 vs 0.833 Sharpe) but a US
investor is much wealthier holding v2. The fold-3 (2020–2025) Sharpe of 0.327 reveals
the structural weakness: in the post-COVID equity bull, over-weighting bonds is
catastrophic in raw returns.

**NOT permanently closed.** Conditions for revisit:
- A sustained bear market materialises (2008-style) where IEF allocation compounds
  dramatically: MinVar's fold-1 Sharpe of 0.96 (during the dot-com/GFC era) shows
  the strategy's genuine value in adverse regimes.
- The 0.267 correlation makes MinVar the most ensemble-eligible strategy ever found;
  if v2 achieves Criterion-1 significance AND both CIs improve, a v2+MinVar ensemble
  could be explored.
- Grid exhausted (4 configs). Do NOT re-tune without a new data regime.
